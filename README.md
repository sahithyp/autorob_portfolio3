# Steve's Moonwalk

For my P3 portfolio submission, I taught Minecraft Steve to moonwalk. He glides backwards across the grass while his legs move as if he were walking forward, then goes into some jazz hands, a spin, and a little disco before the dance loops. His antenna also pops up on the beat.

My code works out where every arm and leg is at each moment, and the page draws Steve from that. Press the music button and watch him dance.
