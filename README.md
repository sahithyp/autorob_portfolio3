# Steve's Moonwalk 🕺

![Steve mid-dance](media/preview.png)

I taught a Minecraft robot to dance, and yes, he can moonwalk. 🌙 Steve glides backwards across the grass while his legs swear they're walking forward, then he throws in some jazz hands, a spin, and a little disco before looping back around. He even has an antenna that pops up on the beat. Don't ask why. It just feels right.

Under the hood, my code figures out where every blocky arm and leg is at every moment, and the page you see draws it. Hit the music button, watch him go, and don't be surprised if your head starts bobbing. 🎶
